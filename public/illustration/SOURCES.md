# Illustration prototype

`complete.webp` and `complete.png`: AI-generated architectural painting approved by the user, 2026-11-15.
`shell.webp` and `shell.png`: edited empty-building variant of that painting, preserving composition as closely as possible.

These are conceptual images, not architectural construction drawings. Browser animation uses masked regions, opacity transitions, perspective linework and pan/zoom. It is not a 3D model or a separately rendered construction-frame sequence.
